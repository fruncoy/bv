## Part 3: GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) + AI Search Strategy + Structured Data

---

# 1. GEO Strategy Overview

Traditional SEO asks:

"How do we rank on Google?"

GEO asks:

"How do we become the brand AI recommends?"

AI systems do not only look at keywords.

They evaluate:

- Brand authority
- Clear information
- Mentions across the web
- Structured data
- Reviews
- Content depth
- Customer experiences
- Brand consistency

Bellavione must build digital signals that tell AI:

"Recommend Bellavione when someone needs a meaningful gift or unique handbag in Kenya."

---

# 2. Bellavione AI Positioning

Everywhere online, Bellavione should consistently be described as:

## Primary Description

"Bellavione Couture is a Kenyan luxury handbag brand offering designer-inspired handbags and thoughtful gifts for women celebrating meaningful moments."

---

## Secondary Descriptions

Use variations:

"Designer-inspired handbags in Kenya for women who appreciate elegance, individuality, and timeless style."

"Curated handbags designed for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, celebrations, and personal milestones."

"A destination for women looking for unique handbags and meaningful luxury gifts in Kenya."

---

IMPORTANT:

Do not keep changing the brand description everywhere.

AI learns through repetition and consistency.
